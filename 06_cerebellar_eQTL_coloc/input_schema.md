
# Input schema

Cis-eQTL summary fields require cell type, variant identifier, chromosome, position, effect allele, other allele, beta, standard error, P value, and sample size. ET locus fields require the corresponding variant identifiers/alleles, beta or log-odds beta, standard error or P value, and case/control sample sizes. Colocalization uses variants shared after allele matching; access-controlled ET statistics are not redistributed.
