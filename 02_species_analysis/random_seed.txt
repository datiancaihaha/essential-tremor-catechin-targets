Species MR-PRESSO analyses used 10,000 simulation draws. The fixed base seed was
20260902. A deterministic analysis-specific seed was derived from the GWAS
accession, instrument tier, and sensitivity-model label; the exact seed and RNG
kind are recorded in species_mr_presso.csv for every estimable analysis. The
first eligible analysis was repeated after the parallel run, and the full
recorded result row was identical (see species_mr_presso_determinism_check.csv).
Primary IVW and other non-simulation estimators are deterministic.
